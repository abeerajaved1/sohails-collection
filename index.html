Our Story

About Sohail's Collection

Sohail's Collection started with one simple idea: a good bag should survive your whole week without looking tired. We focus on bags for men and boys, from everyday backpacks to sharp office briefcases and weekend travel bags.

Every bag in our collection is picked for three things: strong stitching, sensible pockets and a clean, confident look. Whether you are heading to class, to the office or out of town, there is a bag here that fits your day.
